how it works

no demo. it's wired.

01

Scan

The lab pulls the wallet's last few hundred transactions from Solana through Helius and decodes every swap: what it bought, how much SOL, how long it held, what it made.

02

Grow

Those habits pick one of six builds. The echo is born with 1 paper SOL and the lab starts watching the original wallet on-chain.

03

Echo

When the original swaps, its echo answers within seconds at the same fill price the original got, sized by how much of his wallet he put in. Every decision is logged with the reason.
